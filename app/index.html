Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — but free-roam stays open after the story, so almost everything can be cleaned up afterwards.

  1. 1 Play the story 60–80 hours

    Finish the 5 chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go — progress already counts toward the 100% checklist. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see the missable warning below).

  2. 2 The 100% checklist ~100 hours

    After the credits the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion — but robberies, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests need everything. You need any 70 of the 80 challenges.

  3. 3 Strangers & Freaks and cleanup ~10 hours

    Finish the 8 stranger strands that count, the character questlines, and whatever one-off trophies are left. Most of these pop on the way through steps 1 and 2.

  4. 4 Vice Got a Price! the platinum

    Earn every other trophy and the platinum is yours. No multiplayer, no DLC required.

⚠ The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as potentially missable: during Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks out to the balcony at Jason's house. Follow her and accept. It disappears after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required — do it to be safe.

Save often, never cheat on this run

There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots). Using a cheat disables trophies and saving. Optional missions can disappear over time, but PowerPyx notes there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs, so a missed one never locks the platinum.

About this guide

Guides are written in our own words for this guide. Two trophies (Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin) have no confirmed trigger yet; both are marked as unconfirmed rather than guessed at.
